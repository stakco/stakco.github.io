Stakco — Who Buys What & Why

Stakco is a platform for creating, sharing, and experiencing rotating multi-layer puzzles — as physical prints, interactive web content, or projected installations.

Stakco Puzzle

A rotating multi-layer puzzle made of 2–3 concentric circular plates.
Rotate each ring to align the pieces and complete an image. Works as printed paper, interactive online, or projected installations.

Stakco Stencil

A reusable template that defines a puzzle's layer structure and cut pattern.
Choose or purchase a stencil, then apply your own artwork to create unique puzzles.

Stakco Play

Interactive puzzles in the browser
Publish puzzles online — anyone can play instantly via a link. No install needed.
Players Free

What is it?
Puzzles hosted on play.stakcos.com or your custom subdomain. Players open a link and solve puzzles directly in their browser on any device.
Who is it for?
Content creators, educators running online courses, businesses distributing branded campaigns, or anyone sharing puzzles digitally.
What's included?
Unlimited puzzle albums, custom subdomain (yourname.stakcos.com), engagement analytics, and shareable links with instant access.
