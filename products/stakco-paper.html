What is Stakco Paper?
The physical object

A flat disc, 20 cm across, made of laminated matte board with transparent rotary layers joined by a central eyelet. Each layer turns independently — users rotate them to align the image and solve the puzzle. No batteries, no screen, no app.

The design & print process

Design in Stakco Studio (free, browser-based). Add the Print module to export a print-ready file. Then choose production: print at home on card stock, or order professionally printed, packaged units through Stakco’s print partners.

Think of it as a puzzle you can hand to someone — a gift, a giveaway, a classroom handout, a branded keepsake. It works anywhere. It needs nothing. It lasts.

Frequently asked questions
What is a “stencil” and do I need design experience?
A stencil is a ring layout template — it defines how many concentric layers your puzzle has and how they cut. You choose one from a library in Studio; the software maps your image to the ring structure automatically. No design experience needed.
What is the Print module and is it a subscription?
The Print module is a one-time add-on you activate in Stakco Studio — not a subscription. It unlocks high-resolution export of your puzzle as a print-ready file. You purchase it once and it remains active. See the Paper pricing page for the current price.
How difficult is home assembly?
Straightforward. The export includes cutting lines and step-by-step instructions. You need scissors (or a craft knife), card stock, and a paper fastener. Most people finish in 10–15 minutes. Laminating the sheets before cutting significantly improves durability and feel.
Is it safe for children?
Professionally printed units have no sharp edges, no detachable small parts, and a flush-set eyelet. Layers rotate with smooth, low-resistance motion. Designed for ages 5 and up. For children under 3, the eyelet fastener is not recommended.
What is the minimum order for professional printing?
No minimum. You can order a single professionally printed unit. Volume pricing applies at 10, 100, and 1,000 units. For bulk orders with hard event deadlines, contact partnerships@stakcos.com early — we’ll confirm feasibility based on your date.
Can I remove Stakco branding?
Yes. White-label options are available for professional print orders — the puzzle surface and packaging carry only your branding. Available for qualifying orders. Contact partnerships@stakcos.com to discuss.
Who does the professional printing?
Professional printing is coordinated through Stakco’s print partners in Singapore. Stakco manages the order, quality, and delivery — you don’t contact the printer directly. For large or time-sensitive orders, we communicate lead times and constraints upfront before you confirm.
Can I sell puzzles I design?
Yes. With the Publish module add-on, list your designs on the Stakco Shop marketplace. Buyers can purchase as a digital file to print themselves, or as a professionally printed unit. You keep 75% of every sale — Stakco takes 25%.
