What is Stakco Paper?

Stakco Paper is the physical form of Stakco puzzles — printed with rotating concentric layers that you physically turn to solve. Each puzzle is a flat, lightweight disc with interlocking plates that slide over each other.

Design your puzzle in Stakco Studio, export with the Print module, and produce it — at home with a standard printer, or professionally through a print service. Available in multiple materials, sizes, and form factors.

How it works
Create in Studio → Export with Print module → Print & assemble

Design your puzzle artwork in Studio, choose a stencil for the layer structure, then export a high-resolution print-ready file. Print it yourself or order through a professional print service.

Frequently asked questions
Can I print at home?
Yes. The Print module exports high-resolution files you can print on a standard home printer. You'll need to cut and assemble the layers — instructions are included in the export.
What materials are available for professional printing?
Standard card stock, laminated card, recycled paper, and specialty options. Material availability may vary by print partner and region.
Can I order in bulk?
Yes. Professional printing supports bulk orders with volume pricing. Ideal for events, merchandise runs, or classroom sets.
Can I sell printed puzzles I design?
Yes — with the Publish module you can list your designs on the Stakco Shop marketplace for others to purchase.
How durable are the puzzles?
Durability depends on the material. Laminated card stock puzzles are reusable and handle regular use well. Standard paper is best for single-use or light handling.
