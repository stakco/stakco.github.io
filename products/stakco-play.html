How it works
1
Design in Stakco Studio — free
Stakco Studio is the free browser-based tool for creating rotating ring puzzles. Upload an image, set your ring layout and difficulty, preview in real time. No design experience needed.
2
Add the Play module to distribute
The Play module is the subscription that lets you distribute puzzles online. Add it to your Studio account when you’re ready to go live. Your puzzles go live at play.stakcos.com or your custom subdomain.
3
Share via link — players start instantly
Copy the puzzle link and share it anywhere — social media, email, QR code, embedded on your site. Anyone who clicks starts playing immediately. No account, no download, no barrier.
4
Track, update, grow
Monitor completions, time-on-puzzle, and popular content from Studio. Update or replace distributed puzzles at any time without breaking existing links. Build albums, run series, schedule releases.

Frequently asked questions
Is it really free to play? Where’s the catch?
play.stakcos.com is free forever — the daily puzzle, every album, no account, no app, no ads. We make money when someone falls in love with a puzzle and orders the printed disc, or when a venue books an installation. The free app is the front door, not a trial.
Can I publish my own puzzles on Play?
Yes — for brands, educators, and creators, by invitation. Publishing uses Stakco Studio (our content tool) and the Play module subscription. You get your own branded subdomain (your-name.play.stakcos.com), albums, and engagement analytics. Tell us about your channel →
Does it work on Chromebooks, iPads, and old phones?
Yes. If the browser can open a web page, it can run a puzzle. No plugins, no downloads. Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge — all fine. Tested on school Chromebooks, iPads, and standard Android phones.
Can I edit a puzzle after I’ve shared the link?
Yes. Edit, replace, or reorder published puzzles any time from Studio. The link never changes — visitors see the updated version automatically.
