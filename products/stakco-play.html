What is Stakco Play?
The puzzle format

Rotating concentric rings — each layer turns independently. Players rotate them to align the image and solve the puzzle. It's the core Stakco mechanic, running in any browser.

The creation tool

Stakco Studio is the free design tool where you build puzzles. Choose your image, set the ring layout, configure difficulty. When you’re ready, the Play module publishes it online instantly.

The platform

Published puzzles live at play.stakcos.com or your own branded subdomain. Organise them into albums, track engagement, update content anytime — all from Studio.

Think of it as your own puzzle channel — create, publish, and share interactive rotating puzzles with anyone in the world, via a simple link. No installation, no accounts for your audience, no technical setup.

Frequently asked questions
Do players need an account or app?
No. Anyone with the link can play immediately — no sign-up, no download, no account required. This is by design: zero friction between sharing a puzzle and someone playing it.
What is Stakco Studio and do I need to pay for it?
Stakco Studio is the browser-based tool where you design puzzles. It is free to use — create as many puzzles as you like at no cost. You only need a paid module (like Play) when you want to publish them online or add advanced features.
What is a "module" and what does the Play module include?
A module is an add-on you activate within your Studio account to unlock specific capabilities. The Play module unlocks online publishing: your puzzles go live at play.stakcos.com or your custom subdomain, with albums, analytics, and marketplace listing included.
What does a custom subdomain mean?
It means your puzzles have their own branded web address. Instead of appearing at play.stakcos.com, they appear at yourname.play.stakcos.com — your name, your branding. No technical setup is required; it's configured automatically when you activate the Play module.
Can I embed puzzles on my own website?
Yes. With the Dev module add-on, you can embed any published puzzle as an interactive widget on any website, learning platform, or app. This uses a standard embed snippet — one line of code. The Dev module is separate from the Play module and priced on the pricing page.
Does it work on school Chromebooks or older devices?
Yes. Stakco Play runs in any modern browser without plugins or downloads — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge are all supported. It works on Chromebooks, iPads, Android tablets, and standard school-issued devices. If the browser can open a web page, it can run a Stakco puzzle.
Is player data collected? Is it PDPA / GDPR compliant?
Players do not create accounts and are not required to provide personal information to play. Analytics visible to creators cover aggregate engagement metrics (completion rates, time-on-puzzle) — not individually identifiable data. For specific data handling documentation for institutional or educational use, contact hello@stakcos.com.
Can I update or change a puzzle after publishing?
Yes. Published puzzles can be edited, replaced, or reordered at any time from Studio. Existing share links continue to work — players visiting the old link will see the updated version automatically.
