What is Stakco Play?

Stakco Play is the online platform where puzzles come to life. Publish your puzzle creations from Stakco Studio and share them with anyone — players open a link and start solving directly in their browser on any device.

Host puzzles on play.stakcos.com or set up a custom subdomain (yourname.play.stakcos.com) for a branded experience. Organise puzzles into albums, track engagement, and update content anytime.

How it works
Create in Studio → Publish with Play module → Players solve at play.stakcos.com

Design your puzzle in Stakco Studio (free), then use the Play module to publish it online. Share the link — players don't need an account or app to play.

Frequently asked questions
Do players need an account?
No. Anyone with the link can play immediately — no sign-up or download required.
Can I embed puzzles on my website?
Yes — with the Dev module you can embed interactive puzzles as widgets on any web page.
How do I get a custom subdomain?
The Play module includes custom subdomain setup. Choose your name and it's live at yourname.play.stakcos.com.
Can I update puzzles after publishing?
Yes. Published content can be updated, replaced, or reorganised at any time from Studio.
