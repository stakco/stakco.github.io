What is Stakco Light?

Stakco Light is a physical installation that combines a tangible rotary structure — nested plates and rings on smooth railing tracks with grab handles — with projected puzzle imagery. Visitors physically rotate the layers to solve the puzzle, creating an immersive hybrid experience where digital content meets real tactile interaction.

Content is managed remotely through Stakco Studio, so you can update, rotate, or schedule new puzzles without touching the hardware. Ideal for public venues, sensory therapy rooms, and any space that benefits from calming, engaging visual experiences.

How it works
Create in Studio → Deploy with Light module → Projected on-site

We install the projection hardware at your location. You manage content through Studio — create new puzzles, schedule rotations, and monitor the display remotely. The system runs independently once installed.

Frequently asked questions
How is it mounted?
The rotary structure can be wall-mounted, placed on a horizontal surface, or set up as a tabletop unit. It's designed to fit comfortably in most indoor spaces.
What are the dimensions and weight?
The standard rotary structure measures up to 120 cm in diameter and approximately 50 mm in height, weighing around 15 kg. Custom dimensions can be crafted to suit specific space requirements.
Does it play music?
Yes. Therapeutic music and ambient soundscapes play during puzzle interaction, creating a calming multi-sensory experience. Audio can be customised or muted.
Does it need internet?
Yes. A stable internet connection is required for smooth operation — the system fetches puzzle content and streams rotation data for real-time analytics.
Is it suitable for therapy and special needs?
Absolutely. Stakco Light is already used in schools for children with autism and in therapy centres for sensory engagement. The combination of gentle visuals, therapeutic audio, and hands-on interaction supports relaxation and cognitive stimulation.
What happens after the warranty period?
After the first year, extended service plans are available. The hardware is designed for continuous operation with minimal maintenance.
