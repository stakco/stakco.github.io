What is Stakco Light?
The physical part

Nested aluminium rings on smooth railing tracks, with grab handles. Users physically rotate each layer to solve the puzzle. No screen, no buttons — just hands and motion.

The electronic part

An ultra-short-throw projector maps puzzle imagery directly onto the ring surface. As rings rotate, the projected content responds in real time. A web camera tracks ring positions. Controlled locally via Android TV and a standard TV remote.

The software part

Stakco Studio is the browser dashboard where you manage all content remotely — change puzzle themes, schedule rotations, adjust audio. No technician required. No site visit needed for updates.

Think of it as a permanent interactive exhibit — not a screen, not a kiosk. Something visitors can touch, rotate, and return to. Calming enough for a therapy room. Striking enough for a hotel lobby. Meaningful enough for a museum floor.

Frequently asked questions
How much space does it need?
The standard unit is up to 120 cm in diameter and 50 mm deep. For overhead projection, a ceiling clearance of at least 2.8 m is recommended. The system works best in controlled or dim ambient light — a corner, alcove, or dedicated room is ideal, though open lobby placement is possible. We assess all of this during the free pre-quote site visit.
What does installation involve? Do we need to close the space?
Installation typically takes one full day. The immediate work area needs to be clear, but the rest of your space can remain operational. Our team handles all mounting, cabling, and calibration. Before we leave, we walk your designated contact through the Studio dashboard so you can manage content independently from day one.
Does it need internet? What happens if the connection drops?
A stable broadband connection is required for content updates and remote management. If the connection drops temporarily, the installation continues running on the last-loaded content — it does not go blank or stop working. Reconnection restores remote control automatically.
Can we update content ourselves? Do we need Stakco each time?
You manage all content through Stakco Studio — a browser-based dashboard. Change puzzle themes, schedule content rotations, and adjust audio from any device, at any time. No technical background needed. Stakco is not involved in day-to-day content changes.
How do users control the volume and puzzle content?
Yes. Stakco Light runs on Android TV hardware built into the smart projector — included with every installation. Users can control the puzzle directly using a standard TV remote: shuffle puzzles, load the next track, and adjust or mute volume using the buttons they already know. No app download, no touchscreen, no setup. For deeper control — scheduling, theme changes, content uploads — the full Stakco Studio dashboard is available from any browser. Custom audio tracks can also be uploaded for branded or clinical environments.
Is it safe for children and unsupervised use?
Yes. The rings move on smooth, low-resistance tracks with no sharp edges, no pinch points, and minimal force required to rotate. The installation at AWWA School is in daily unsupervised use with children. For clinical environments, we can discuss specific configuration requirements.
What happens after the 1-year warranty?
Extended service plans are available and quoted individually based on your usage and location. The hardware is designed for continuous operation with minimal maintenance. Contact partnerships@stakcos.com for service plan rates.
Can we use our own branding and puzzle designs?
Yes. Puzzle content is fully customisable through Stakco Studio — upload your own imagery, set brand colours, and create puzzle tracks specific to your institution or campaign. White-label options (removing Stakco branding entirely) are available for qualifying partners.
