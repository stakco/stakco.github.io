Privacy Policy

Introduction

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) explains how Stakcos (“Stakcos,” “we,” or “us”) handles user information in connection with the Stakco Watch mobile application (the “App”). Stakco Watch is a design-focused app that simulates watch movements and is intended purely for creative and visual use.

We value your privacy and are committed to being transparent about our data practices. This Policy applies solely to the App and does not cover other services, websites, or applications that we do not operate.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data from users. The Stakco Watch app operates entirely on your device and does not transmit any information to our servers.

  • No account creation required
  • No collection of name, email, location, or IP address
  • No cookies or trackers used
  • No access to your photos, camera, microphone, or contacts

Analytics and Third Parties

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or AI processing services within the App.

Children’s Privacy

The Stakco Watch app is not intended for children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected, no information from children is knowingly gathered.

Security

Although the App does not transmit or store personal data, we take care to ensure that it functions securely on your device and does not perform background data collection or unauthorized access.
