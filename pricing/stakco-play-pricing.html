Stakco Play — Commercial Pricing & Revenue Model

Recurring revenue from digital puzzle publishing — module subscriptions, add-ons, and platform economics
Proprietary puzzle technology · exclusively licensed for commercial use
Executive Summary

Stakco Play is the digital player platform for Stakco puzzles. Players access puzzles for free via the browser — the revenue model is built around creators who publish, not players who consume. Creators purchase the Studio Play module (subscription) to deploy interactive puzzle albums to their custom subdomain. Additional modules (AI, Dev, Publish) create layered upsell opportunities.

1. Business Model Overview

Stakco Play follows a creator-pays, player-free model. The platform earns recurring revenue from creators who need the Play module to publish puzzle albums. Players never pay — they access puzzles via a link in any browser. This creates a flywheel: more creators → more content → more players → more creators.

User type Pays? What they get
Player Free forever Solve puzzles via link — no account, no app, no install
Creator (free) Free Design puzzles in Stakco Studio — limited creation, no publishing
Creator (publisher) Subscription Play module unlocks publishing: albums, subdomain, analytics, all play types
Creator (power user) Multiple subs Play + AI + Dev + Publish — full platform access for content businesses

3. Album Play Types
All play types are included with the Play module — no per-type charges. These drive creator engagement and retention.
Play type How it works Use case
Free-play All puzzles in the album are available immediately. Players choose freely. Portfolios, galleries, browse-and-solve collections
Sequential Puzzles unlock one after another. Player must solve each to proceed. Courses, story-driven series, progressive difficulty
Day-driven A new puzzle unlocks each day. Players return daily to see the next one. Advent calendars, daily challenges, drip content
Time-driven Puzzles become available at specific scheduled times. Event countdowns, timed campaigns, live competitions
