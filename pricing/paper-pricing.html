Stakco Paper — Commercial Pricing & Cost Structure

Per-unit pricing, bulk tiers, and reseller terms for printed Stakco puzzles
Executive Summary

Stakco Paper is a physical rotating puzzle product — printed concentric layers that users physically turn to solve. Puzzles are designed digitally in Stakco Studio, then produced via professional printing. This document details per-unit costs, bulk pricing, and reseller terms.

1. Product Description
Attribute Detail
Form factor Flat disc with 2–3 concentric rotating layers. Thin profile (<1 mm assembled)
How it works Each plate layer rotates independently. The user aligns the layers to complete the image or pattern
Standard sizes 20 cm width (standard). Custom sizes available on request
Materials Laminated matte board backplate, transparent rotary layers, joined with a durable eyelet
Packaging Clear sleeve (standard), branded box (premium), or unpackaged (bulk)
Shelf life Unlimited — no batteries, no expiry. Laminated versions are reusable
Weight ~15–40 g depending on size and material
