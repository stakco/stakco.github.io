Unique Features

Discover what makes Stakco truly revolutionary

Unlimited Rotatable Layers

Stakco puzzles can consist of a minimum of 2 layers but unlimited numbers of layers to enhance the challenge and fun.

This flexibility allows for creating deeply engaging experiences tailored for different age groups, themes, or cognitive skill levels.

Multiple Form Factors

Stakco physical puzzles are available in multiple sizes, produced in various materials ranging from thin paper-type puzzles to thick acrylic plate puzzles, large scale wall-mount or table-mount structures, and more.

Unlimited Segmentation Styles

Visible puzzle segments can have any shape of your choice or any custom shape, including composite shapes.

This unlocks creative possibilities for storytelling, educational activities, and artistic expression—transforming each puzzle into a truly personalized and memorable experience.

Multiple Platforms

Currently available as digital puzzles (Stakco Play), printed physical puzzles (Stakco Paper), and hybrid projection puzzles (Stakco Light).

We are working on launching multiple other platforms, and the Stakco experience can be embedded in many places.

Single-Unit Puzzle Structure

You'll never lose a piece — this innovative, single-unit design features integrated, rotatable layers for a seamless puzzle experience.
