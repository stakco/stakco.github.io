Accessibility Innovation

Audio Puzzles for BLV People

Audio Stakcos offers a new way to engage blind and low-vision individuals through playful sound-based puzzles. It shows potential as both an accessible experience and a tool to strengthen auditory processing—similar to how visual puzzles enhance cognitive and spatial abilities.

This breakthrough technology opens up entirely new possibilities for inclusive puzzle experiences, ensuring that visual impairment doesn't limit access to engaging, challenging entertainment.

Inclusive & Accessible Puzzle Design

Developing inclusive puzzle formats that utilize alternative sensory inputs to engage users, broadening accessibility and interaction possibilities. Our commitment to universal design ensures that Stakco puzzles can be enjoyed by people of all abilities.

By prioritizing accessibility from the ground up, we're creating experiences that don't just accommodate differences—they celebrate them and create richer interactions for everyone.

Cognitive Training

Empowering Minds with Video Stakco

Video Stakcos offers a novel way to boost cognitive skills through interactive play. Because the puzzle content changes every second, users must stay focused, anticipate motion, and adapt quickly—exercising memory, attention, and coordination in real time.

This dynamic format holds potential for cognitive training, especially for individuals with attention difficulties or those undergoing neuro-rehabilitation. It transforms a playful activity into an opportunity to challenge and sharpen the mind.

Supporting Emerging Artists

Stakco produces puzzles with images crafted by emerging artists. We add value to their art pieces and their artworks add value to Stakcos. This symbiotic relationship creates new revenue streams for artists while enriching our puzzle library with diverse, original artwork.

By partnering with emerging artists, we're helping to democratize art distribution and create sustainable income opportunities in the creative economy.
