Stakco Light

Sensory puzzle room at AWWA School @ Bedok

In collaboration with AWWA School @ Bedok in Singapore, a special education school for students aged 7 to 18 with autism, we developed a specialised Stakco Light installation guided by their therapists' feedback. The system was set up in the school's multi-sensory room to provide a calming, tactile, and interactive experience.

From concept to classroom

The installation features physical nested ring plates on railing tracks with grab handles — designed for accessibility. Students physically rotate the plates while a projector maps the corresponding digital puzzle layers onto the structure in real time.

Designed for teachers, inspired by students

Teachers create curated puzzles using Stakco Studio, tailoring content to fit their curriculum or individual student needs. The experience is enhanced with therapeutic audio, fostering a soothing and sensory-friendly environment.

"The combination of tactile interaction and visual feedback made it an engaging experience for students — the physical rotating mechanism kept them focused while supporting upper-body movement."

Ongoing study — 2026

AWWA School @ Bedok is conducting a structured classroom trial from March to May 2026 with selective classes to evaluate learning outcomes, sensory engagement, and motor development.

Stakco Paper

84 custom puzzles for a wedding in India

For a client's wedding celebration in India, we produced 84 individual Stakco Paper puzzles, each featuring a unique custom image from the couple's memories. All puzzles were designed, printed, assembled by hand, and shipped from our studio in Singapore to the event venue.

Automation meets craft

Our in-house Stakco CLI tool automated the entire pipeline — from image cropping and layer generation to printable PDF export. A post-processing script compiled all 84 puzzle assets in one pass.

Precision printing and assembly

Transparent layers and cardboard backplates were produced by a professional printing supplier. Every puzzle was then assembled by hand — each layer manually fed through the cutting machine, precisely aligned, and secured with eyelet pressing.

Stakco Paper

Puzzle-driven booth engagement at OGA 2023

Vivo Asia Engineering & Trading, a Singapore-based telecom, IT, and ELV systems integrator, deployed custom Stakco Paper puzzles at their booth during the 19th Oil, Gas and Petrochemical Engineering Exhibition (OGA 2023) in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. The exhibition ran from 13–15 September under the Singapore Pavilion.
