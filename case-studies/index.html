Stakco Light

Sensory puzzle room at AWWA School @ Bedok

In collaboration with AWWA School @ Bedok in Singapore, a special education school for students aged 7 to 18 with autism, we developed a specialised Stakco Light installation guided by their therapists' feedback. The system was set up in the school's multi-sensory room to provide a calming, tactile, and interactive experience.

From concept to classroom

The installation features physical nested ring plates on railing tracks with grab handles — designed for accessibility. Students physically rotate the plates while a projector maps the corresponding digital puzzle layers onto the structure in real time, creating a seamless blend of physical and digital interaction.

Designed for teachers, inspired by students

Teachers create curated puzzles using Stakco Studio, tailoring content to fit their curriculum or individual student needs. Puzzles sync automatically to the display, and teachers can switch between puzzles, mix pieces, or solve them via a simple interface. The experience is enhanced with therapeutic audio, fostering a soothing and sensory-friendly environment.

Stakco Paper

84 custom puzzles for a wedding in India

For a client's wedding celebration in India, we produced 84 individual Stakco Paper puzzles, each featuring a unique custom image from the couple's memories. All puzzles were designed, printed, assembled by hand, and shipped from our studio in Singapore to the event venue.

Automation meets craft

Our in-house Stakco CLI tool automated the entire pipeline — from image cropping and layer generation to printable PDF export. A post-processing script compiled all 84 puzzle assets in one pass.

Precision printing and assembly

Transparent layers and cardboard backplates were produced by a professional printing supplier. Every puzzle was then assembled by hand at our workspace — each layer manually fed through the cutting machine, precisely aligned, and secured with eyelet pressing.

Interactive engagement at community events

We deployed printed Stakco puzzles during community gatherings and business events. The puzzles proved to be effective ice breakers and entertainment pieces — not just a game, but a medium that carries a message, a story, or a brand.

Participants naturally engaged with the tactile rotating mechanism, sparking conversation and connection. Stakco Play

Cognitive training with Video Stakco

Video Stakcos extend the puzzle concept from static images to dynamic video content. Because the puzzle changes every second, users must stay focused, anticipate motion, and adapt in real time — exercising memory, attention, and coordination simultaneously.

Potential for neuro-rehabilitation

Early research suggests video puzzles demand significantly higher mental engagement than static formats. This makes them a promising tool for cognitive training, particularly for individuals with attention difficulties or those undergoing neuro-rehabilitation. The format transforms playful interaction into measurable cognitive exercise.
