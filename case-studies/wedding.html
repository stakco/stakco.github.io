Stakco Paper

84 custom puzzles for a wedding in India

For a client's wedding celebration, we produced 84 individual Stakco Paper puzzles — each featuring a unique custom image from the couple's memories — designed, printed, assembled by hand, and shipped from Singapore to India.

Automation meets craft

Our in-house Stakco CLI tool automated the entire pipeline — from image cropping and layer generation to printable PDF export. A post-processing script compiled all 84 puzzle assets in one pass.

Precision printing and assembly

Transparent layers and cardboard backplates were produced by a professional printing supplier. Every puzzle was then assembled by hand at our workspace — each layer manually fed through the cutting machine, precisely aligned, and secured with eyelet pressing.
