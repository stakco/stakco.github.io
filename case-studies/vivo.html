The concept

Vivo Asia Engineering & Trading, a Singapore-based telecom, IT, and ELV systems integrator, participated in the 19th Oil, Gas and Petrochemical Engineering Exhibition (OGA 2023) held from 13–15 September at the Kuala Lumpur Convention Centre, under the Singapore Pavilion. The exhibition attracted hundreds of industry professionals over three days.

To stand out among traditional exhibition booths, Vivo Asia deployed custom Stakco Paper puzzles as an interactive engagement tool. Rather than relying solely on brochures and presentations, the puzzles gave visitors a reason to stop, interact, and spend time at the booth.

Booth execution

Each puzzle was custom-designed to feature Vivo Asia's products and systems — including maritime communication equipment, CCTV systems, and engineering services. The concentric rotating layers of each puzzle showcased different aspects of their offering.

The key interaction mechanic: solving a puzzle revealed a hidden QR code at the centre, leading directly to Vivo Asia's website. This turned a simple booth giveaway into an active discovery experience — visitors had to engage with the product imagery to unlock the digital link.

Pilot results

This deployment was run as a pilot to measure user engagement at trade shows. The puzzles successfully attracted visitors to the booth, extended dwell time, and created a memorable interaction compared to conventional print materials.

Engagement pilot — 2023

This was Stakco's first trade show deployment as a booth engagement tool. The format proved effective for drawing foot traffic and creating a tactile, memorable brand interaction in a professional B2B setting.
