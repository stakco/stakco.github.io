Shipping & in trial

Products you can buy or pilot today. The research base behind them.

Tangible interface Shipping

Stakco Light — physical rings + projection

Physical rotating rings driving a projected digital image. Studied at AHLab and now in daily classroom use at AWWA School for sensory engagement and motor learning. Structured trial running March–May 2026. Product page →
Multimedia format Prototype

Segmented audio puzzles

An auditory version of the ring puzzle for blind and low-vision users. Segmented audio tracks replace visual layers; solving means aligning rhythm and spatial audio. Working prototype, seeking pilot partners.
