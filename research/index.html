Research & Innovation

Pushing the boundaries of puzzle interaction

Stakco sits at the intersection of tangible interface design, accessibility research, and interactive media. We partner with universities and institutions to explore what puzzles can become.

Breakthrough

Video Stakco — puzzles with moving content

We extended the Stakco concept from static images to dynamic video. The puzzle content changes every second, requiring continuous visual tracking, temporal alignment, and active attention. From a research perspective, this opens new directions in studying cognitive load, attention span, and pattern recognition in dynamic environments.

Early feedback suggests video puzzles demand significantly higher mental engagement than static formats, making them a promising tool for real-time problem-solving research and perceptual learning studies.

Core research areas

What we're exploring

Active research directions across multimedia, accessibility, and platform innovation.

Multimedia

Segmented multimedia puzzles

Investigating how segmented visual, audio, and video content can be layered across rotating rings to create novel puzzle experiences. The core mechanism — dividing media into concentric segments that must be aligned — is the foundation of all Stakco formats.
Interface Design

Tangible interface innovation

Exploring how physical rotating mechanisms interact with digital content. Stakco Light is a tangible user interface — physical plates drive a projected digital experience. We study how different input modalities affect engagement, comprehension, and therapeutic outcomes.
Accessibility

Audio puzzles for blind & low-vision users

We're developing Audio Stakcos by adapting the puzzle concept to the auditory domain. Segmented audio waveforms replace visual layers, creating an interactive multi-track puzzle that relies on spatial audio, rhythm recognition, and auditory pattern matching.
Inclusive Design

Accessible puzzle formats

Developing puzzle formats that use alternative sensory inputs — tactile feedback, haptic cues, and audio guidance. Our goal is to make the Stakco experience genuinely accessible to users with diverse abilities, not as an afterthought but as a core design principle.
Platform breadth

Runs on more than you'd expect

The Stakco puzzle engine is designed to be platform-agnostic. We've deployed it in unexpected places to test interaction patterns.

Wearable

Galaxy Watch

Experimental Stakco apps running on smartwatches. The rotating bezel maps naturally to the ring-turning mechanic. This research explores micro-interactions and gesture-based puzzle solving on ultra-small screens.
Smart TV

Android TV

Stakco puzzles on the big screen, controlled via remote, voice, or companion app. This investigates how large shared displays can create collaborative puzzle-solving experiences in living rooms and public spaces.
Applied Research

Artist collaboration

We produce puzzles featuring artwork by emerging artists. The puzzle format adds interactive value to their pieces, while their work enriches the Stakco library. This research explores interactive puzzles as a new medium for artistic expression and distribution.
Immersive Tech

AR & spatial computing

Early-stage exploration of Stakco puzzles in augmented reality and spatial computing environments. Multi-dimensional puzzle interaction in 3D space represents the next frontier of the format.
