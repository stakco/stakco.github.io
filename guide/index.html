Stakco — Who Buys What & Why

Every Stakco customer starts with a free Studio account. Paid modules unlock specific capabilities. This maps real customer needs to the modules they'd purchase and the audience they represent.

Key Concepts

Stakco Puzzle — A rotating multi-layer puzzle made of 2–5 concentric circular rings.
Users rotate each ring to align the pieces and complete an image. Available as printed paper, interactive online, or projected installations.

Stakco Stencil — A reusable template that defines a puzzle's layer structure and cut pattern.
Creators design or purchase stencils, then apply their own artwork to produce unique puzzles.

The Stakco Platform

Stakco Studio — Free web-based tool for designing puzzles using stencils.
Paid modules unlock additional capabilities — printing, online publishing, projection, AI generation, marketplace selling, and developer integration.

Stakco Play — Online platform where published puzzles are played in the browser.
Creators share interactive puzzles via links or custom subdomains. Players solve them directly — no install required.

Stakco Shop — Marketplace for modules, stencils, printed puzzles, and community designs.
Users browse and purchase add-ons to power up Studio. Creators list and sell their own work here.
